# Brag Plan: PopPay

## What is this app?
PopPay is a high-speed, zero-fee peer-to-peer UPI checkout & payment gateway for Indian merchants and creators, powered by automated bank and FamApp email alert matching and instant real-time SSE payment push.

## The angle
Traditional payment gateways take 2–3% of every transaction and make you wait 2 days for settlements. PopPay lets merchants receive direct peer-to-peer UPI payments with zero gateway cuts, automatic paise-suffix matching, and instant zero-latency checkout verification.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on obsidian canvas: "STOP PAYING 2% ON EVERY UPI PAYMENT." A red fee cut counter gets struck through, replaced by "0% GATEWAY FEES. 100% YOURS."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Pulse dynamic checkout: glowing QR, unique paise discriminator (`₹100.02`), and live SSE connection.
- The automated matching magic: customer pays via FamApp / UPI, backend worker catches the alert in 0.5s.
- Instant checkout flip: green verification checkmark, verified UTR, zero manual refresh needed.

## Outro / punchline
"Direct to bank. Zero middlemen. Built for India's fastest creators."
PopPay logo with glowing signature gradient and `poppay.tech`.

## User flow worth showing
1. Checkout opens with dynamic UPI QR & ₹100.02 payable amount.
2. Customer completes payment on UPI / FamApp.
3. Server-Sent Events (SSE) stream triggers real-time "PAID" state with transaction ID.
